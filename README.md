# Amazon-Retail-Sales-Dashboard

A retail analytics dashboard focused on sales performance, customer behavior, product efficiency, and regional business growth.

## Overview

This dashboard analyzes:

sales and revenue trends
customer activity
product performance
order volume
regional performance
target vs actual achievement
Business Objective
To analyze revenue, profit, customers, products, orders, and regional performance to identify business trends and opportunities.

## KPI Highlights

Total Sales
Total Transactions
Total Customers
Average Quantity per Transaction
Average Customer Age
Rolling 3-Month Sales
Sales Last 30 Days
Sales Target Value
Target Achievement %
Target Difference

## Tools Used

Power BI
SQL
DAX
Power Query

## Key Features

Sales overview
Customer segmentation
Product performance analysis
Order and revenue tracking
Target achievement monitoring
Regional trend analysis

## Business Insights

-Sales trends highlight top-performing regions and product categories
-Customer and order analysis support retention and conversion strategies
-Target tracking reveals business gaps and opportunity areas
-Profitability analysis reveals market potential
